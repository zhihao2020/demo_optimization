\begin{abstract}
Rising shares of wind and photovoltaic generation require coordinated hourly dispatch of thermal units, battery storage, compressed-air energy storage (CAES) and the grid. This paper considers weekly price-taker scheduling of a multi-energy plant whose nonlinear dynamics are given by a Modelica functional mock-up unit (FMU). Device limits, inventories and interchange capacity induce a state-dependent hybrid action region that cannot be written in closed form. Physics-constrained hybrid TD3 (PC-HybridTD3) generates continuous thermal and battery set-points together with a CAES mode--magnitude pair already inside a computable joint feasible-support model \(\Afhat(s)\), applies GiveSafe as a residual physical check, and updates the critics only on executed transitions. The method is evaluated in closed loop on the 0903 Sysplorer FMU against a price-aware heuristic and a rolling mixed-integer linear programme on four seasonal final-holdout weeks. Ranking uses the weekly net objective \(CC\) (\(10^6\)~CNY/week; lower is better). PC-HybridTD3 improves mean \(CC\) by \(7.48\) million CNY/week relative to the rolling MILP and by \(10.78\) million CNY/week relative to the heuristic, with zero unserved energy and zero hard-constraint violations, at \(3.8\)~ms per step versus \(225\)~ms for the MILP.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Wind and photovoltaic (PV) variability couple thermal generation, battery energy storage (BESS), compressed-air energy storage (CAES) and grid interchange through a shared bus and shared inventories~\cite{zhou2023iesdrl,yang2024planning}. A thermal ramp, a battery charge and a CAES mode switch all change the same power balance. This paper studies weekly price-taker operation of a thermal--BESS--CAES plant~\cite{emmanuel2020market,pei2024pricetaker} under a time-of-use (TOU) tariff and an emissions-trading charge. The objective is the expected operating cost
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\min_{\pi}\;
\mathbb{E}\Biggl[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1} C_t\Biggr],
\quad
C_t
&=
C_t^{\mathrm{grid}}
+C_t^{\mathrm{thermal}}
+C_t^{\mathrm{carbon}}\\
&\quad
+C_t^{\mathrm{curt}}
+C_t^{\mathrm{uns}}
+C_t^{\mathrm{deg}}
+C_t^{\mathrm{su}}.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:C}
\end{equation}
Wind, PV and load are exogenous, so the controller must coordinate the dispatchable assets rather than schedule any one of them in isolation.

Mixed-integer linear programmes (MILP) and model-predictive control make constraints explicit~\cite{cui2025ghtd3,ceusters2021mpcrl}, but on a nonlinear multi-physics plant they typically convexify thermodynamics or incur a non-trivial online solve. Deep reinforcement learning (DRL)~\cite{sutton2018rl,lillicrap2016ddpg,fujimoto2018td3,haarnoja2018sac} learns a closed-loop policy and, once trained, evaluates a command in a single forward pass. Continuous DRL schedulers for integrated energy and CAES--PV plants~\cite{zhou2023iesdrl,li2025iesdrl,wu2024pricing,sun2024constrained,dolatabadi2023caes} typically act in a regular box, or restore feasibility by clipping after the fact. Parameterized-action methods factor a discrete choice and a continuous magnitude~\cite{hausknecht2016pas,masson2016paa,fan2019hybrid}, yet they do not place a same-hour policy on a plant-wide, state-dependent support. Constrained RL has been applied to integrated energy dispatch~\cite{sun2024constrained}, and a CAES energy hub has been scheduled with safe RL~\cite{garmroodi2023caes}. Hierarchical option and goal methods plan across time scales~\cite{cui2024caesbess,cui2025ghtd3,jendoubi2023hier,ochoa2024bidding}. The remaining gap is heterogeneous device actions generated on a joint feasible region and executed on a nonlinear FMU.

The set of commands the FMU will execute, \(\AFMU(s)\), is not available analytically. A computable oracle supplies a joint support \(\Afhat(s)\) that approximates it. Physics-constrained hybrid TD3 (PC-HybridTD3) emits
\(a_t=(u_t^{\mathrm{th}},u_t^{\mathrm{bat}},m_t,z_t)\)
already on \(\Afhat(s_t)\), with GiveSafe as a residual physical check~\cite{garcia2015safe,sun2024constrained}:
\begin{equation}
a_t\sim\pi_\theta(s_t)\;\Rightarrow\; a_t\in\Afhat(s_t),
\label{eq:support-gen}
\end{equation}
rather than \(a_t=\Pi(\pi_\theta(s_t))\). Only transitions advanced by the FMU enter the economic Bellman update.

The paper makes three contributions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item A hybrid action that treats thermal generation and BESS as continuous commands and CAES as a mode--magnitude pair, avoiding projection of a disconnected envelope onto idle.
\item A joint feasible-support model \(\Afhat(s)\) that encodes ramps, inventories, CAES restrictions and interchange capacity in action generation, with GiveSafe as the last physical screen.
\item Closed-loop evaluation on the FMU, with a 24~h forecast in the state, against a heuristic, a rolling MILP and a parametric PSO on four seasonal final-holdout weeks.
\end{enumerate}

Section~\ref{sec:problem} formulates the scheduling problem. Section~\ref{sec:method} presents PC-HybridTD3. Section~\ref{sec:results} reports the case study. Section~\ref{sec:conclusion} concludes.

\subsection{Scheduling model and constraints}
\label{sec:constraints}

Let \(t=0,\ldots,T-1\) with \(T=168\). Renewable and thermal injection are non-negative; battery and CAES charge is positive and discharge is negative; grid import is positive. The electric bus closes with curtailment and unserved residual,
\begin{equation}
P_t^{\mathrm{th}}+P_t^{\mathrm{bat}}+P_t^{\mathrm{caes}}
+P_t^{\mathrm{w}}+P_t^{\mathrm{pv}}+P_t^{\mathrm{grid}}
=
P_t^{\mathrm{load}}.
\label{eq:balance}
\end{equation}
Thermal power \(P_t^{\mathrm{th}}=u_t^{\mathrm{th}}P_{\mathrm{cap}}^{\mathrm{th}}\) obeys
\begin{equation}
P_{\mathrm{th}}^{\min}\le P_t^{\mathrm{th}}\le P_{\mathrm{th}}^{\max},
\qquad
\bigl|\Delta P_t^{\mathrm{th}}\bigr|\le R^{\mathrm{th}},
\label{eq:th}
\end{equation}
with \(P_{\mathrm{th}}^{\min}=50\)~MW, \(P_{\mathrm{th}}^{\max}=150\)~MW and \(R^{\mathrm{th}}=0.15\)~p.u.\ per hour. Battery power \(P_t^{\mathrm{bat}}=u_t^{\mathrm{bat}}P_{\mathrm{cap}}^{\mathrm{bat}}\) updates SoC with discharge efficiency \(0.85\), subject to
\begin{equation}
SOC_{\mathrm{bat}}^{\min}\le SOC_{\mathrm{bat},t}\le SOC_{\mathrm{bat}}^{\max},
\label{eq:batsoc}
\end{equation}
with bounds \((0.1,0.9)\).

The CAES electric command occupies the disconnected envelope
\begin{equation}
u_{\mathrm{caes}}
\in
[-1,-0.33]\;\cup\;\{0\}\;\cup\;[0.86,1],
\label{eq:legal}
\end{equation}
which encodes minimum compressor and expander loading. Mode locks and inventory margins shrink the set further. The CAES state exposed to the controller is
\begin{equation}
x_t^{\mathrm{CAES}}
=
[s_{\mathrm{gas}},\,s_{\mathrm{hot}},\,s_{\mathrm{cold}},\,p,\,T,\,\ldots].
\label{eq:caes-x}
\end{equation}
Grid exchange satisfies
\begin{equation}
P_{\mathrm{grid}}^{\min}\le P_t^{\mathrm{grid}}\le P_{\mathrm{grid}}^{\max},
\label{eq:grid}
\end{equation}
with limits \(\pm500\)~MW. Interchange couples the three physical commands,
\begin{equation}
P_t^{\mathrm{grid}}
=
P_{\mathrm{cap}}^{\mathrm{th}} u_t^{\mathrm{th}}
-
P_{\mathrm{cap}}^{\mathrm{bat}} u_t^{\mathrm{bat}}
-
P_{\mathrm{cap}}^{\mathrm{caes}} u_t^{\mathrm{CAES}}
-
R_t,
\label{eq:pgrid-u}
\end{equation}
where \(R_t\) is the residual of wind, PV and load. Equation~\eqref{eq:pgrid-u} is the interchange slice of the decoder;~\eqref{eq:balance} is the bus used in~\eqref{eq:C}.

\subsection{Economic objective and MDP}
\label{sec:mdp}

With purchase and sale energies \(E_t^{\mathrm{buy}}=\tau_s[P_t^{\mathrm{grid}}]_+\) and \(E_t^{\mathrm{sell}}=\tau_s[P_t^{\mathrm{grid}}]_-\), the cost~\eqref{eq:C} comprises TOU trading and a soft interchange-contract excess, thermal fuel and O\&M, an ETS carbon charge, curtailment and unserved penalties, battery wear and CAES start-up. Policies are compared on \(CC=\sum_t C_t\).

The problem is the finite-horizon MDP \(\mathcal{M}=\langle\mathcal{S},\Afhat(\cdot),P,r,\gamma\rangle\) with \(\gamma=0.99\). The state
\begin{equation}
s_t=[x_t,\,f_{t:t+23},\,\lambda_{t:t+23},\,z_t]
\label{eq:state}
\end{equation}
concatenates the FMU physical vector \(x_t\), a 24~h forecast of wind, irradiance, temperature and load, the TOU path, and a short bookkeeping vector \(z_t\) (observation dimension \(166\)). The hybrid action is
\begin{equation}
a_t=(u_t^{\mathrm{th}},u_t^{\mathrm{bat}},m_t,z_t),
\quad m_t\in\{D,I,C\},\; z_t\in[0,1].
\label{eq:action-hyb}
\end{equation}
Device-level support is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{dev}}(s)
=
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{tp}}(s)\times
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{bat}}(s)\times
\bigcup_{k\in\mathcal{K}(s)}
\bigl(\{k\}\times\mathcal{M}_k(s)\bigr),
\label{eq:adev}
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal{K}(s)\) is the admissible CAES mode set and \(\mathcal{M}_k(s)\) the current magnitude interval. Writing \(\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{grid}}(s)\) for the commands that meet~\eqref{eq:grid},
\begin{equation}
\Afhat(s)
=
\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{dev}}(s)\cap\mathcal{A}_{\mathrm{grid}}(s).
\label{eq:support}
\end{equation}
The physical command is the continuous triple \((u^{\mathrm{th}},u^{\mathrm{bat}},u_{\mathrm{caes}})\), with
\begin{equation}
u_t^{\mathrm{CAES}}
=
\begin{cases}
\underline{u}_D(s_t)+z_t\bigl[\overline{u}_D(s_t)-\underline{u}_D(s_t)\bigr], & m=D,\\
0, & m=I,\\
\underline{u}_C(s_t)+z_t\bigl[\overline{u}_C(s_t)-\underline{u}_C(s_t)\bigr], & m=C.
\end{cases}
\label{eq:decode}
\end{equation}
The learning reward is a scaled negative of \(C_t\), with light inventory shaping; ranking uses \(CC\).

\section{Physics-Constrained Hybrid TD3}
\label{sec:method}

PC-HybridTD3~\cite{lillicrap2016ddpg,fujimoto2018td3,fan2019hybrid} is a single-timescale actor--critic on a parameterized hybrid action~\cite{hausknecht2016pas,masson2016paa}: thermal and battery heads are continuous, while CAES is a mode--magnitude pair because~\eqref{eq:legal} is disconnected. GiveSafe is adopted as a residual physical check~\cite{garcia2015safe,sun2024constrained}. Fig.~\ref{fig:algorithm} summarises the loop.

\subsection{Joint feasible-support decoder}
\label{sec:decoder}

At each hour the actor is decoded onto \(\Afhat(s)\). Illegal CAES modes are removed by a Boolean mask \(M(s)\in\{0,1\}^3\),
\begin{equation}
\tilde\ell
=
\ell + \log\bigl(M(s)+\varepsilon\bigr),
\label{eq:mask}
\end{equation}
so that \(\mathrm{softmax}(\tilde\ell)\) is supported on \(\mathcal{K}(s)\). Evaluation and the TD3 target take \(m'=\arg\max_m\tilde\ell(s')\). With thermal and battery boxes \((u^{\mathrm{th}}_{\mathrm{lo}},u^{\mathrm{th}}_{\mathrm{hi}})\), \((u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{lo}},u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{hi}})\), ratings \(P_T,P_B,P_C\) and interchange band \((G_{\min},G_{\max})\), the CAES grid window is
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\underline{u}_C^{\mathrm{g}}
&=
\bigl(P_T u^{\mathrm{th}}_{\mathrm{lo}}-P_B u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{hi}}-R_t-G_{\max}\bigr)/P_C,\\
\overline{u}_C^{\mathrm{g}}
&=
\bigl(P_T u^{\mathrm{th}}_{\mathrm{hi}}-P_B u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{lo}}-R_t-G_{\min}\bigr)/P_C.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:caes-window}
\end{equation}
Each interval \(\mathcal{M}_k(s)\) is intersected with this window and empty modes are masked. After \(u^{\mathrm{CAES}}\) is decoded by~\eqref{eq:decode}, thermal is remapped onto the residual interval
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\underline{u}^{\mathrm{th}\star}
&=
\bigl(G_{\min}+P_B u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{lo}}+P_C u^{\mathrm{CAES}}+R_t\bigr)/P_T,\\
\overline{u}^{\mathrm{th}\star}
&=
\bigl(G_{\max}+P_B u^{\mathrm{bat}}_{\mathrm{hi}}+P_C u^{\mathrm{CAES}}+R_t\bigr)/P_T,
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:th-window}
\end{equation}
and battery is remapped given \((u^{\mathrm{th}},u^{\mathrm{CAES}})\),
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\underline{u}^{\mathrm{bat}\star}
&=
\bigl(P_T u^{\mathrm{th}}-P_C u^{\mathrm{CAES}}-R_t-G_{\max}\bigr)/P_B,\\
\overline{u}^{\mathrm{bat}\star}
&=
\bigl(P_T u^{\mathrm{th}}-P_C u^{\mathrm{CAES}}-R_t-G_{\min}\bigr)/P_B.
\end{aligned}
\label{eq:bat-window}
\end{equation}
GiveSafe then tests predicted next-state inventories and residual twin constraints that cannot be written as intervals~\cite{garcia2015safe,sun2024constrained}. Thus \(\Afhat(s)\) is a computable approximation of \(\AFMU(s)\).

\subsection{Physical-only TD3 update}
\label{sec:td3}

Twin critics \(Q_{\phi_1},Q_{\phi_2}\) score the executed triple \((u^{\mathrm{th}},u^{\mathrm{bat}},u_{\mathrm{caes}})\). With \(Q=\min(Q_{\phi_1},Q_{\phi_2})\),
\begin{equation}
y
=
r+\gamma(1-d)\,Q_{\bar\phi}\bigl(s',u^{\mathrm{th}\prime},u^{\mathrm{bat}\prime},u_{\mathrm{caes}}'\bigr).
\label{eq:td3-y}
\end{equation}
The target actor is deterministic in the mode, \(m'=\arg\max_m\tilde\ell_{\bar\theta}(s')\); clipped Gaussian noise is added only to the magnitude,
\begin{equation}
\tilde z'=\mathrm{clip}\bigl(z'(s')+\varepsilon,0,1\bigr),
\label{eq:target-mag}
\end{equation}
and \(\tilde z'\) is re-decoded with \(\mathcal{M}_{m'}(s')\). The delayed actor maximises \(Q_{\phi_1}\) on the decoded hybrid action. Mode non-differentiability is handled by Straight-Through Gumbel--Softmax.

Executed FMU transitions enter the economic replay \(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{B}}\) and drive the critic and actor losses. Rejected proposals leave the plant unmoved and are stored only for audit. Algorithm~\ref{alg:pc-hybridtd3} and Table~\ref{tab:hyper} summarise the procedure.

\begin{algorithm}[!t]
\caption{PC-HybridTD3}
\label{alg:pc-hybridtd3}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Actor \(\pi_{\theta}\), twin critics, replay \(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{B}}\)
\State Warm-start with random feasible hours on \(\Afhat(s)\)
\For{each episode on a training week}
  \For{\(t=0\) to \(T-1\)}
    \State Decode CAES, thermal and battery on \(\Afhat(s)\)
    \State Accept \(a^{\star}\) by GiveSafe; otherwise terminate
    \State Step the FMU; store the transition in \(\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{B}}\)
    \State Target: \(\arg\max\) mode, noise on \(z'\) only, re-decode at \(s'\)
    \State Update critics; delayed Gumbel actor
  \EndFor
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsection{Ablations}
\label{sec:ablation}

Two encodings are not used by the live policy. Continuous projection maps a CAES latent by \(u_{\mathrm{caes}}=\Pi_{\mathrm{bands}}(\tanh(z))\), sending the illegal gap to idle. A static-support hybrid retains the mode mask~\eqref{eq:mask} but decodes magnitude on device boxes, without the sequential grid-coupled decoder. Fig.~\ref{fig:action-rep} shows the three representations; only (c) is executed in the case study. The CAES head is the hybrid sub-action of a plant-wide policy.

\section{Case Study}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Experimental protocol}
\label{sec:setup}

All methods run on the 0903 Sysplorer FMU, the 2026 monthly 110~kV tariff, and the same traces. Each episode lasts \(T=168\)~h. Training uses the 36 stratified training weeks; checkpoints are not selected on the final test weeks. The reported comparison uses the second test week of each quarter---weeks 12, 25, 38 and 51---which were not used as the default development eval week. Inventories reset at each weekly start. PC-HybridTD3 is trained for \(4\times10^{5}\) physical hours. The main comparison uses a perfect 24~h forecast. Holdout rollouts ran on a workstation with an Intel Core i9-14900K CPU (24 cores, 32 threads) and one NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080 GPU (16~GB). The heuristic and the rolling MILP decide on the CPU; PC-HybridTD3 evaluates the trained actor on the GPU. Decision times in Table~\ref{tab:kpi} and Fig.~\ref{fig:runtime} exclude the FMU step.

The baselines are a price-aware heuristic, a rolling MILP on a linear energy surrogate, and a six-parameter PSO price policy. Rule and MILP are deterministic. PSO searches \(12\times25\) FMU weeks on each evaluation week and then deploys the best parameter vector as a closed-loop policy; Table~\ref{tab:kpi} reports that subsequent one-step decision time, not the search. Ranking uses weekly \(CC\) from~\eqref{eq:C}. Unserved energy, hard-constraint violations and decision time complete the table.

\subsection{Safety validation}
\label{sec:learn}

A 20\,000-step near-boundary stress on the 0903 plant recorded 18\,444 Oracle-legal actions and zero post-step hard failures. Stage~C (30k physical hours) passed gates C1--C5 with zero false-safes. Stage~D (400k hours) recorded zero post-step hard violations. Table~\ref{tab:safety} summarises the chain. Fig.~\ref{fig:training} is the training log, recorded every 500 physical hours: step reward stays in a bounded band, and critic loss drops by several orders of magnitude in the first \(5\times10^{4}\) hours. No holdout \(CC\) is computed during training.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Weekly multi-energy scheduling on an FMU plant requires thermal generation, BESS, CAES and the grid to be moved together inside a state-dependent support that is not a product of device boxes. PC-HybridTD3 generates heterogeneous commands on a computable approximation \(\Afhat(s)\), screens them with GiveSafe, and learns only from executed hours. On four seasonal final-holdout weeks of the 0903 plant, PC-HybridTD3 improves mean weekly \(CC\) by \(7.48\) million CNY relative to a rolling MILP and by \(10.78\) million CNY relative to a price-aware rule, with zero unserved energy, at \(3.8\)~ms/step. The magnitude intervals \(\mathcal{M}_k(s)\) include oracle margins, inventories are reset each week, and September--December buy prices still follow the last published month.
